Common questions

A product I scanned wasn't found.
Helkost carries every product Open Food Facts has given a NOVA rating — 1.1 million of them — and looks up anything else online as you scan it. If a barcode is missing from Open Food Facts entirely, nobody has added it yet. You can add it yourself at openfoodfacts.org, and Helkost's Contribute screen helps you send in the labels you have photographed.
Why does a product with the Keyhole still show NOVA 4?
Because they measure different things. The Keyhole (Nyckelhålet) rates fat, sugar, salt and fibre. NOVA rates how industrially processed a food is. A product can do well on one and badly on the other, and Helkost shows you both rather than hiding the contrast.
Is NOVA the same as healthy?
No. NOVA describes processing, not healthiness. Helkost is not medical advice — see a doctor or a dietitian for anything that matters to your health.
My diary didn't appear on my other device.
Syncing goes through your own iCloud. Check that you are signed into the same Apple Account on both, and that Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Drive is on. The first sync can take a few minutes.
What do the AI features cost?
Every day you get 40 product explanations, 3 recipe suggestions, 3 homemade alternatives and 2 calorie-target estimates for nothing. Helkost Plus (79 kr a month) raises those and adds the cooking chat and calorie estimates from a photo. Scanning, the diary, HealthKit, the shopping list and iCloud sync are free and always will be.
I have hit today's limit.
Limits reset at midnight UTC. They exist because each of those features costs money to run — not to push you towards paying. If ordinary daily use keeps reaching a limit, tell me and I will raise it.
How do I cancel Helkost Plus?
In Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription — only Apple can. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I paid but the features are still locked.
Tap Restore Purchases in More → AI. A subscription belongs to your Apple account rather than to a device, so this is also how you get it back on a new phone.
Why is the app so large?
The whole product database is inside it, so scanning works with no network at all — in a shop basement, on aeroplane mode, anywhere.
How do I delete everything?
Deleting the app clears the device. For the synced copy, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Helkost.

Privacy

No sign-up, no email address, no profile, and no analytics. Your diary and your recipes live on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, where nobody else can read them. The AI features do use a server, and the account it keeps for you is a random number that holds your daily usage counts and nothing else — no name, no email, no questions, no answers. The full privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored and for how long, and the terms of use cover the subscription.
