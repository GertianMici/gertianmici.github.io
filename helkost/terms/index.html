What Helkost is

Helkost helps you see how processed a food is, keep a food diary, and cook things yourself instead of buying them. It uses the NOVA classification and nutrition data published by Open Food Facts.

Helkost is not medical advice. NOVA describes how processed a food is; it does not describe whether a food is healthy for you. Calorie figures, and especially calorie estimates from a photograph, are approximations. Nothing in the app diagnoses, treats or manages any condition. If your health depends on what you eat — an allergy, diabetes, an eating disorder, pregnancy, medication that interacts with food — talk to a doctor or a dietitian. Do not rely on this app for that.

The product data comes from a public database. Open Food Facts is contributed to by the public. An ingredient list can be out of date, incomplete, or wrong, and a manufacturer can change a recipe without anyone updating it. Always read the physical label if it matters — particularly for allergens.

Helkost Plus

Some features are included; some need a subscription.

Free, every day:

  • 40 product explanations
  • 3 recipe suggestions from a craving
  • 3 homemade alternatives to a product
  • 2 AI calorie-target estimates

Scanning, the diary, HealthKit, the shopping list, recipes and iCloud sync are free and always will be. The limits above apply only to the features that call a language model, because those cost money each time.

Helkost Plus, every day:

  • 300 product explanations
  • 40 recipe suggestions
  • 40 homemade alternatives
  • 20 AI calorie-target estimates
  • 60 cooking chat messages
  • 12 calorie estimates from a photograph

Limits are per day and reset at midnight UTC. They exist to keep the service affordable, not to upsell you: the free allowances are set so that ordinary daily use of the app never reaches them.
