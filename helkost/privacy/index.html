Helkost

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Helkost has no server, no account, and no analytics. Everything it knows lives on your iPhone or in your own iCloud.

What Helkost stores, and where

On your device. The product database ships inside the app — 1,135,564 products, read-only. Your language, daily energy target and body details are kept in the app's own settings.

In your iCloud, not ours. These sync through CloudKit to your private iCloud database:

  • your food diary and the portions you log
  • your scan history
  • saved recipes, and the meal plan you build from them
  • your shopping list
  • the prices you record for products
  • the ingredient labels you have read with the camera
  • your cooking chat

That is your Apple account, governed by Apple's privacy policy. Nobody else can read it, and neither can the developer of this app.

In the iOS Keychain. One credential, and only if you choose to use the feature that needs it: your own OpenAI API key. It is stored on that device only, excluded from backups, and deletable from inside the app.

In Apple Health, if you allow it. With your permission Helkost reads the energy you have burned, so the daily target reflects how active you have been, and writes what you log so your food appears alongside everything else. Health data is exchanged with HealthKit on the device. It is never sent anywhere, and it is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

What leaves your device

Two things, both only when you ask for them.

Barcode lookups. When you scan a product the app does not carry, Helkost asks Open Food Facts about that barcode. The barcode is all that is sent — no identifier, nothing about you, nothing about your diary. Helkost only ever reads from Open Food Facts; nothing you write is ever sent there.

The AI features, if you turn them on. They are off until you enter your own OpenAI API key, which is stored in the iOS Keychain on that device only, excluded from backups, and never transmitted anywhere except to OpenAI. When you ask a question, only the product or recipe text the question is about is sent, billed to your own OpenAI account. Nothing about you, your diary or your history goes with it. See OpenAI's privacy policy. Deleting the key in More → AI stops all of it.

Camera and photos

The camera is used to scan barcodes and to read ingredient labels. Photographs of meals are yours to pick from your photo library or take with the camera; the picture is analysed and kept with your diary entry. Helkost does not have access to the rest of your photo library — iOS shows you its own picker and hands over only the image you choose.

A meal photo is analysed for a calorie estimate only if you have turned on the AI features with your own key, in which case that image goes to OpenAI and nowhere else. Otherwise it stays on your device and in your iCloud.

What Helkost does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address. The OpenAI key above is optional, is yours, and is billed to your own account with them.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking of any kind, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No selling of anything to anyone.

Children

Helkost collects nothing from anyone, at any age.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything on the device. To remove the synced copy, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Helkost and delete the data there.

Not medical advice

Helkost describes how processed a food is, using the NOVA classification, and shows nutrition figures published by Open Food Facts. NOVA describes processing, not healthiness. Calorie estimates from a photograph are estimates, and the app shows them as a range with its assumptions visible for that reason. Nothing in the app is medical or dietary advice. Talk to a doctor or a dietitian about anything that matters to your health.
