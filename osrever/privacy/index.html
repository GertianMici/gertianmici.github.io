OsreveR

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026

OsreveR is private by default. It has no server, no account and no analytics, and it does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal information.

The microphone

OsreveR records short audio clips, and only while you are actively playing a round. iOS asks your permission the first time, and you can take it back at any point in Settings → OsreveR → Microphone.

All of the recording, reversing, playback and match-meter scoring happens entirely on your device. Clips are written only to the app's temporary storage and are removed when you start a new round. Nothing is uploaded, and no copy is kept anywhere else.

What leaves your device

Nothing. OsreveR contains no networking code at all — it never talks to any server, mine or anyone else's, and it works exactly the same in aeroplane mode.

What OsreveR does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email address.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking of any kind, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No selling of anything to anyone.

Children

OsreveR collects nothing from anyone, at any age.

Deleting your data

Starting a new round clears the previous recordings. Deleting the app removes anything left.
