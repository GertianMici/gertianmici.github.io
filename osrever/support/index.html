OsreveR

Support

Say it backwards. Then flip it. Record a word, hear it reversed into gibberish, and dare a friend to copy the sound.

Getting help

Email gertian.mici@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and which iOS version you are on, and what you expected to happen — it makes a fix much faster.

How to play

  1. Player 1 records a word — a single clear word or short phrase.
  2. Hear it reversed. OsreveR flips the audio. It sounds like gibberish.
  3. Player 2 mimics it. Listen as many times as you like, then copy the reversed sound.
  4. Flip and reveal. OsreveR reverses Player 2's attempt and plays it next to the original, so everyone can judge the match.

It is a pass-and-play game: one device, two or more players taking turns.

Common questions

Nothing gets recorded when I tap the record button.
OsreveR needs the microphone. Check Settings → OsreveR → Microphone and turn it on. If you declined the permission the first time, that is the only place to grant it again.
The match meter gave me a low score, but it sounded right.
The meter compares the shape of the two recordings — how the loudness rises and falls — not the words themselves. It is not speech recognition, and it is meant as a laugh rather than a verdict. Background noise and how far you hold the phone both move the number around.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Everything happens on the device, and the app has no networking code at all. It works the same in aeroplane mode.
Can I save or share a recording?
No. Clips live in the app's temporary storage for the round you are playing and are removed when you start a new one.
My recording sounds quiet or clipped.
Record in a quiet room, hold the phone about a hand's width away, and say one clear word rather than a long sentence. Short, distinct words reverse into the funniest gibberish and are the easiest to mimic.
Which iPhones does it work on?
Any iPhone running iOS 16 or later.
How do I delete my recordings?
Start a new round, which clears the previous ones, or delete the app to remove anything left.

Privacy

OsreveR has no server, no account and no analytics, and your recordings never leave your phone. The full privacy policy spells out what that means.
